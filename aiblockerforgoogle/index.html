What it does

A focused block for one specific problem.

AI Blocker for Google is built around a narrow job: remove Google's AI Overview module from search result pages while leaving the rest of the search experience intact.

1

Removes AI Overviews

When Google inserts an AI Overview at the top of search results, the app hides that panel so links are visible first.

2

Keeps normal results

Search results, source links, ads, maps, snippets, and other standard Google Search page elements are not the target.

3

Runs locally

The blocking logic runs in your browser. It does not require an account, background server, analytics SDK, or tracking service.

Why it exists

Less generated summary, more direct source material.

Search is most useful when it quickly gets you to the sources you want to inspect. This app removes the generated answer block so the rest of the results page can do that job.

Without AI Blocker AI panel visible
  • Generated overview takes the first result position.
  • Source links start lower on the page.
  • The page favors a summary before original material.
With AI Blocker AI panel hidden
  • Regular Google results move back into focus.
  • You can scan source pages faster.
  • The search page stays closer to the classic link-first flow.

What data we collect

AI Blocker for Google runs locally in your browser and hides AI Overview elements on Google Search result pages. The app does not send your searches or page contents to Fe Studio.
