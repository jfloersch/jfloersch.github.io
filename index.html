Engineer

Jared Floersch

I'm a mechatronics engineer based in Minneapolis. My work has ranged from robotic musculoskeletal simulators and exoskeleton actuators to independent apps like Scrolless, OpenBookshelf, and WatchPomo.

This site is where I keep my writing, selected work, and the small products I'm making through Fe Studio.
