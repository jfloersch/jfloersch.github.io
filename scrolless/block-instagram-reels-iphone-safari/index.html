What Scrolless can block on Instagram

Scrolless can block distracting Instagram destinations such as Reels and other feed-like surfaces in Safari. It can also clean suggested posts and algorithmic clutter from feeds you choose to keep.

The goal is not to punish social connection. The goal is to block the feed while keeping profiles, search, messages, posting, and useful links reachable.

Why use Safari instead of the Instagram app?

The native app is designed to be smooth, sticky, and always close by. Safari adds useful friction. You still get the web version of Instagram, but you can use a browser extension like Scrolless to block specific surfaces that are hardest to control.

That makes Safari a practical middle ground: less addictive than the native app, but not as drastic as quitting Instagram completely.
