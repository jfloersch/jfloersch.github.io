AgentPace

Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 2026  ·  Fe Studio

AgentPace has no analytics, telemetry, ads, backend, or account system. Usage history stays local on your Mac.

Who we are

AgentPace is developed and published by Fe Studio, an independent software studio. You can reach us at jared@festudio.net.

What data we collect

Fe Studio does not collect, transmit, store remotely, sell, or share personal data, usage data, analytics, crash reports, or telemetry from AgentPace.

How AgentPace works

AgentPace is a native macOS menu-bar app that reads local Claude Code and Codex login state only so it can fetch your usage directly from Anthropic and OpenAI usage endpoints. Fe Studio does not receive these requests or operate a server for AgentPace.

For Codex, AgentPace resolves the local Codex CLI and reads usage through the Codex app-server. For Claude Code, AgentPace can use its own Claude sign-in stored in an AgentPace-owned macOS Keychain item, an environment token, local Claude credentials, or an optional Claude Code Keychain fallback.

Credentials and tokens

Tokens are never stored in AgentPace settings or local usage history. AgentPace-owned Claude credentials, when used, are stored in the macOS Keychain. Tokens are sent only to the relevant provider usage endpoints needed to refresh your usage data.

Local history

AgentPace stores usage snapshots and refresh errors locally on your Mac for up to 30 days: ~/Library/Application Support/AgentPace/snapshots.jsonl and ~/Library/Application Support/AgentPace/errors.jsonl.

You can delete this local history from AgentPace Settings using Clear local history. This data is not uploaded or synced by AgentPace.

Third-party services

AgentPace does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash reporting SDKs. Usage refreshes communicate directly with Anthropic and OpenAI provider endpoints, and those providers' privacy policies govern data they receive.

Purchase and payment processing is handled by Apple through the App Store. Apple's privacy policy governs any data collected during purchase or restore flows. Fe Studio never receives your payment information.

Children's privacy

AgentPace does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13. Because Fe Studio does not collect data through AgentPace, the app is designed to avoid collecting children's personal information.

Changes to this policy

If we change how AgentPace handles data, we will update this policy and change the effective date above. We will not add data collection without updating this policy first.
