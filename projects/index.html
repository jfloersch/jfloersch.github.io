Projects

A few things I've worked on.

Robotic musculoskeletal simulators

I designed anatomically informed robotic systems intended to replicate human joint behavior for training physical therapists and U.S. Army combat medics. The work combined structure, custom actuation, controls, and the realities of getting full systems built and delivered.

Rehabilitation and industrial exoskeletons

This work included actuator design, nonlinear controls, MATLAB and Python simulation, and sensor fusion for wearer interaction. The interesting part was not any single subsystem but the way the entire device had to behave as a coherent extension of the body.

Transcatheter mitral valve replacement

At Abbott Laboratories I contributed to early transcatheter mitral valve replacement work, including solutions to critical nitinol fatigue failure modes. The work led to patent applications and supported early human clinical trials.

Low-cost oxygen blender

A bCPAP-compatible oxygen blender assembled from standard hospital supplies, reducing the cost of a needed clinical tool from thousands of dollars to a few dollars in material cost. It has been used in hospitals in Cambodia, Nigeria, and Thailand.

Independent apps through Fe Studio

My software work tends to start with a narrow frustration: algorithmic feeds, cluttered reading trackers, or focus tools that keep the phone at the center. Scrolless, OpenBookshelf, and WatchPomo are all attempts to solve those problems with tools that stay small and opinionated.

Selected publications

A Low-Resource Oxygen Blender Prototype for Use in Modified Bubble CPAP Circuits: Results from Design Feasibility Workshops (2022) reported follow-on workshop results showing the device could be assembled reliably by new users.

Ineffective Orifice Area: Practical Limitations of Accurate EOA Assessment for Low-Gradient Heart Valve Prostheses (2021) examined the practical limits of accurately measuring effective orifice area in large-diameter heart valve testing.

Human Gesture Robot Control Using a Camera/Accelerometer-in-Palm Sensor (2021) described a palm-based gesture interface for real-time robot control using computer vision and inertial sensing.

A Low-Resource Oxygen Blender Prototype for Use in Modified Bubble CPAP Circuits (2020) documented a low-cost oxygen blender designed for modified bubble CPAP use in low-resource environments.
